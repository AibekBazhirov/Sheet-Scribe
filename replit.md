
## Manual Integration Note

The user dismissed the Google Sheets connector. 
To enable Google Sheets integration, the following environment variables need to be set:

- `GOOGLE_SHEET_ID`: The ID of the Google Sheet (from the URL).
- `GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_EMAIL`: Email of the Google Cloud Service Account.
- `GOOGLE_PRIVATE_KEY`: Private key of the Service Account.

The Google Sheet must be shared with the Service Account email.
